📌 Executive Overview

FoodDash is built from the ground up to redefine online food ordering by combining lightweight high-speed frontend design with real-time dining features. Incorporating WebGL 3D food exploration, AI-driven macro meal planning, table booking, and group cart syncing, FoodDash serves as a comprehensive modern web ecosystem.

🛠️ Tech Stack & Engineering Architecture

Tier Technology Purpose
